I make short films and documentaries, cut long-form video, and write about both. I'm finishing a B.A. in Film & Media Studies at Washington University in St. Louis, with minors in Writing and the Business of the Arts.

I was president of WUTV, the university's student television station. Currently, I run the Productions for WashU Film Club, producing a film every semester. In my free time, I write for the student newspaper and photograph for fun.
